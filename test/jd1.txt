Research Intern – Democracy and Rule of Law Programme (m/f/d) – Vienna Office, 6 months, starting February 2027

About us
The International Institute for Democratic Governance (IIDG) is an independent think tank based in Vienna, Austria, working on rule of law in Central and Eastern Europe.

About the role
We are looking for a motivated Research Intern to support our Democracy and Rule of Law Programme for six months, starting in February 2027.

Your tasks
• Conduct desk research and literature reviews on judicial independence
• Support data collection and data analysis for our annual index
• Draft short policy briefs and blog posts
• Assist with event management for our spring conference

Requirements
• Currently enrolled in or recently graduated from a Master's programme in political science, law, international relations or related field
• Excellent written and spoken English (C1); German is an advantage
• Strong analytical skills and attention to detail
• Experience with Excel; knowledge of Stata or R is a plus

What we offer
• Monthly stipend of EUR 1,100 gross (full-time, 40 hours per week)
• Hybrid working arrangement
• Mentoring by senior researchers

How to apply
Please send your CV, a cover letter (max. 1 page) and a writing sample in a single PDF to internships@iidg.org with the subject line "Research Intern 2027".
Deadline: 15 December 2026. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
