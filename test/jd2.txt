Junior Data Analyst
Budapest, Hungary | Full-time | Hybrid

Northwind Analytics is looking for a Junior Data Analyst to join our growing Insights team in Budapest.
What you'll do
Build dashboards in Power BI and Tableau for our retail clients
Write SQL queries to extract and clean data
Present findings to stakeholders in clear, non-technical language
Work with the data engineering team on data quality

What we're looking for
Bachelor's or Master's degree in economics, statistics, computer science or similar
1-2 years of experience in a data role (internships count)
Proficiency in SQL and Python
Fluent English; Hungarian is not required
You must have the right to work in the EU; we cannot sponsor visas.

Benefits
Competitive salary (HUF 650,000 - 800,000 gross per month), cafeteria, 25 days holiday, learning budget
